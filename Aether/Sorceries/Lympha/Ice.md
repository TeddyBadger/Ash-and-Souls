### **Overview**

These sorceries are a sub type of the Lympha sorceries, using the water based aether from them to conjure spells that consist of ice.

---
### **Icecrag**

You conjure an icey comet that homes towards your target.

|     **Info**     |   **Value**   |
| :--------------: | :-----------: |
|      Damage      | 1d8 + Insight |
|   Aether Cost    |      16       |
|     Priority     |      14       |
| Attunement Slots |       1       |
| Projectile Speed |   5m / Tick   |
|  Ticks to Cast   |       1       |
|   Action Type    |     Full      |
|  Stat Build-Up   | 10 Frostbite  |

---
### **Ice Storm**

Strike the ground with your staff to conjure an ice storm around you in a 2m radius. This does damage every 2 ticks and can be held to continue the storm.

|     Priority     |            12             |

| Projectile Speed |            N/A            |
|  Ticks to Cast   |             2             |
|   Action Type    |           Full            |
|  Stat Build-Up   | 15 Frostbite per 2 ticks  |
