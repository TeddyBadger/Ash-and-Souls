### **Overview**

These incantations are considered taboo, being used by the followers of chaos to spread madness and destruction across the land of Yarven.

---
### **Frenzied Burst**

Release a concentrated blast of frenzied flame from your eyes. The range of this is 14m.

|   Action Type    |     Full      |

|   Damage Type    |     Fire      |

### **Unendurable Frenzy**

### **Howl of Morgren**

Let out a shriek that lasts 2 ticks. This damages and causes Madness build up to anyone within 2m of you. However, this also provides a buff and debuff to the user. This increases all forms of damage dealt by 1d6, but also reduces all of your DR values by 3 and makes enemies more likely to target you for 2 rounds.
