### **Overview**

These incantations were created by a long forgotten and revered Empyrean who was a master at forming and using the power of light to create large devastating attacks.

---
### **Spira**

Hold your hands together and raise them to the heavens, causing a spiral of light to raise from the ground underneath your target's feet. This works anywhere within 10m of the caster.
