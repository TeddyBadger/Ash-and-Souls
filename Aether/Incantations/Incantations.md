## **Overview**

Incantations are cast using aether, and a conduit that is under the “Seal” category. These require the user to be at a certain level of Resolve to be cast, and scale off of Resolve too. The damage type differs based on the incantation.

Note: Aether costs and damage may be tweaked at a later date if needed due to balancing.
