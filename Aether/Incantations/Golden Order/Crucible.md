### **Overview**

These incantations are a sub type of Golden Order incantations: they harness the power of the ancient crucible knights to cast an array of different versatile incantations.

---
### **Crucible Bloom**

Conjure a large lily on your back and curl up, allowing it to call down a rain of holy light. This rain lasts 4 ticks and effects everywhere in a 2m radius from you.
