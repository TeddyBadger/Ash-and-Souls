### **Overview**

These incantations were created by those who worship The Great Arborea, using holy powers to create incantations offensively or defensively.

---
### **Golden Vow**

Raise your arm to cast a veil of holy protection, giving you and anyone within a 2m radius of you a buff: This allows everyone to add 1d6 damage to all of their attacks and increases everyone's DR by 2 for the "caster's Resolve" amount of turn.
