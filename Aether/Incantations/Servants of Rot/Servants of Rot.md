### **Overview**

These incantations were created by the worshippers of the Goddess of Rot, an outer god that symbolises death, decay and rebirth through the Scarlet Rot.

---
### **Poison Armament**

Applies poison to the weapon in your other hand, or can be cast onto another persons weapon at the cost of 2 further ticks. This allows the attacker to add "Resolve" to the damage they deal and cause an extra 15 poison build up on hit.

### **Scarlet Aeonia**

Grow large wings made of Aeonian butterflies as you rise into the air. The tick following the this, fly anywhere 8m from you and slam down into the ground. The next tick, you explode into a giant, blooming flower that takes the shape of a Scarlet Bud, dealing damage and Rot build up in a 6m radius. This effect lingers for 2 ticks, causing more rot build up to anyone who is still in the area while the flower slowly dissipates.
