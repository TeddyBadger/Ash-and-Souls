### **Overview**

These incantations are a sub type of Flame incantations: they channel the power of messmerfire. This is a unique type of fire that is particularly deadly, and has a deep, blood-red colour. It was created by a demi-god of myth called Messmer, but in today's times, his worshipper's use it to cast the incantations that are said to have been made and used by Messmer.

---
### **Fire Serpent**

Create a ball of messmerfire above your head that shoots a serpent like projectile of messmerfire that homes onto its target 2 ticks after casting the initial ball of messmerfire. The projectile has a maximum range of 6m.
