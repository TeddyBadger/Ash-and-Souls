## **Overview**

Curved greatswords have good reach and flexibility, but not as quick as other swords. They usually deal slower than normal, but heavy hits.

## **Magma Wyrm's Scalesword**

Curved greatsword wielded by Magma Wyrms. The shape resembles a dragon's jaw and is covered in hard scales. It's said these land-bound dragons were once human heroes who partook in dragon communion, a grave transgression for which they were cursed to crawl the earth upon their bellies, shadows of their former selves.
