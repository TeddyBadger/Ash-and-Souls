## **Overview**

Curved greatswords have good reach and flexibility, but not as quick as other swords. They usually deal slower than normal, but heavy hits.

---
## **Omen Cleaver**

Heavy-bladed curved sword of colossal size awarded to Omen as a tool of war. This weapon is made to take advantage of brute strength.
