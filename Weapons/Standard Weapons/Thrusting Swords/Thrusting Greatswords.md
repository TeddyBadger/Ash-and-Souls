## **Overview**

Heavy Thrusting Swords focus on piercing attacks and are best used to counter or follow-up an enemy's attack. Their extra weight packs some extra physical attack power compared to lighter thrusting swords.

---
## **Great Épée**

Large rapier featuring a thin, sharp blade. Commonly used in life or death duels. Like its smaller counterparts, it is made for thrusting attacks, and can be used while guarding with a shield.
