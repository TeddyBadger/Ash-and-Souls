## **Overview**

Straight swords are easy to use weapons that are balanced and versatile. They all can scale off of Might or Grace and tend to be used as a priority weapon, sometimes paired with a shield.

---
## **Longsword**

A straight sword with a double edged blade. The most basic but trustworthy weapon.
