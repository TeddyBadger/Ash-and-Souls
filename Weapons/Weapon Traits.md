## Guarded (+N)

The weapon geometry, balance, and hand protection make it exceptional for defensive redirection: When you make a **parry**, add **+N** to your **parry Test** result.  
This affects only **parry**, not attacks or blocking.

## Unpredictable (+N)

The weapon's articulated striking head moves along an arc that rigid weapons cannot predict or cleanly intercept. When a creature attempts to parry an attack made with this weapon, increase the DN of their parry test by N.

## Unbalanced (-N)

Some weapons are **difficult to control defensively** due to forward weight, uneven mass distribution, or recovery lag when deflecting force. They can hit hard, but are poor at redirecting incoming attacks.

When performing a **parry** with an Unbalanced weapon:  
- You suffer **–N** on your **parry test**.  
- This applies only to **parry**, not to **block** or attack rolls.

## Hooking

Used to **catch**, **drag**, or **bind** an opponent’s guard.  
When you **hit** a target who is currently **Blocking**, the target must make a Might Test** with **DN = 10 + your Might Score**.

- **Failure:** Their **block immediately ends**.
- **Success:** block continues normally.

## Crushing (1dN)

This weapon delivers force that can **break guard on impact**. When you hit a target who is currently **blocking**, roll **1dN**. Reduce the target’s **block DR** by the amount rolled **immediately**, for the **remainder of this round**. This reduction applies **before** damage is resolved. If the block Value is reduced to **0**, the defender’s **block ends immediately**.

### Concealable

The weapon is easy to hide on the body.

- Creatures attempting to **detect if you are armed** have **disadvantage** on relevant Perception checks.
- Drawing this weapon while concealed **does not automatically reveal hostility** (GM decides when tension breaks).

## Intimidating

The weapon’s appearance or reputation provokes caution.

- When you **brandish or ready** this weapon, you gain **advantage** on Intimidation Tests.
- Has **no effect** on creatures immune to fear or without emotional reasoning.

## Silent

The weapon does not produce audible noise when striking flesh or moving through air.

- Attacks with this weapon do **not** produce detectable combat noise unless they strike materials that would create noise, such as metal.
- Useful for ambush and stealth operations.

## Reliable

The weapon’s balance and design make it consistent and trustworthy.

- **Once per round**, you may **reroll** a **damage die** showing **1**, taking the new result.

## Reach

Weapons with **Reach** threaten space **beyond normal melee range**. When a creature **enters your melee range**, you may make **one melee attack against them at the expense of your reaction**. This is called a **Reach Punish**.

- The Punish resolves at the **same priority the creature moves into your melee range.
- You may only perform **one Reach Punish per round, even if multiple creatures enter your range. 

**Close-In Penalty:** If an enemy is **within 1m:

- Your **melee attack tests** with this weapon are at **disadvantage**
- Your **parry tests** with this weapon are at **disadvantage** This penalty applies **only** while the enemy remains within 1m of you.

## **Swift (+N)**

This weapon is very lightweight and easy to handle, making it easy to stay quick on your feet. While wielding this weapon, you gain +N to evasion.

## **Heavy (-N)**

Due to this weapons weight, you lack your standard mobility. While wielding this weapon, you lose N evasion.
## **Quick Slash**

Thanks to the versatility of this weapon, you may spend a half action to do another attack after using a full action to attack initially. If you have not already attacked using a full action, you cannot do this.

## **Double Hit**

Because of the versatility and unique shape of this weapon, when attacking with a full action, you can freely times your damage by 1.5 as long as you are using both hands, due the other part of this weapon hitting the victim again.
