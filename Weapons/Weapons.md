## **Overview**

Every weapon will have a multitude of information and values consisting of their Damage, Damage Type, Weapon Type, Weapon Art, Critical Damage Type, Category, Range, Stat Scaling, Stat Build-Up, Poise Damage and a variety of different Traits. Below are lists of the possible Weapon Types and Damage Types:

Damage Types:
- Physical
- Fire
- Holy
- Lightning
- Magic

Weapon Types:
- Daggers
- Straight Swords
- Curved Swords
- Thrusting Swords
- Greatswords
- Katanas
- Twinblades
- Axes
- Hammers
- Spears
- Halberds
- Reapers
- Whips
- Fists
- Hand to Hand Arts
- Bows
- Staffs
- Sacred Seals
- Perfume Bottles

All weapons in the "Standard" category can be upgraded with normal Smithing Stones and can have their Weapon Art changed by using Ashes of War. There are usually multiple of these weapons across the world, so multiple people can use them. However, "Unique" weapons can be upgraded with Somber Smithing Stones, have a set Weapon Art that is completely unique and there is typically only one of each of these weapons.
