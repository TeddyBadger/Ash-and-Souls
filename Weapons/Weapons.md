Damage Types:

- Lightning
- Magic

Weapon Types:

- Axes
- Hammers
- Spears
- Halberds
- Reapers
- Whips
- Fists
- Hand to Hand Arts
- Bows
- Staffs
- Sacred Seals
- Perfume Bottles
