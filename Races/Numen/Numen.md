## **Overview**

**Little is known about the Numen: They are highly intelligent, their race has adapted to live in the shadows and even though they look ashen, they are not. They are a long-lived race, originating from an unknown land.**

## **Traits**

### **Quick-Footed**

Prerequisites: None.  
Cost: 2 Trait Points.  
Your light and nimble body has prepared you for the worst. You gain +2 to your evasion and you can perform a backstep: this has priority 17 and makes you jump back 2m in 1 tick.

### **Natural Stealth**

Prerequisites: None.  
Cost: 2 Trait Points.  
You no longer create sound when moving and you also gain a +1 to the Stealth skill.

### **Extreme Awareness**

Prerequisites: None.  
Cost: 2 Trait Points.  
You are constantly alert, meaning you are naturally more perceptive and aware of your surroundings. You gain a +1 to the perception skill and you can sense any creature within 5m of you .

### **Surprise Attack**

Prerequisites: None.  
Cost: 2 Trait Points.  
When making an attack on an enemy, if they have not noticed you before the attack is made, you deal an extra 1d6 damage.

### **Perfect Camouflage**

Prerequisites: Quick-Footed, Natural Stealth.  
Cost: 4 Trait Points.  
You may completely conceal yourself at will for a maximum of 2 turns. This can be done at will and only “grace” amount of times per long rest.

### **Lethal Strike**

Prerequisites: Surprise Attack.  
Cost: 3 Trait Points.  
This is an improved version of surprise attack. The extra damage from attacking a clueless enemy is now 2d6.

### **Master of Agility**

Prerequisites: Perfect Camouflage, Lethal Strike.  
Cost: 4 Trait Points.  
Your grace is now capped at 7 instead of 6. This takes 6 trait points to increase from 6 to 7.
