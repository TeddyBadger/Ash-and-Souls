## **Overview**
**You are a special demi-god with the potential to ascend to godhood, chosen by the divine envoys. You have no memories of your childhood, though as the light shines for you, it is clear that your chosen by a higher power.**

## **Traits**

### **Advanced Memory**

Prerequisites: None.  
Cost: 2 Trait Points.  
You may change one of your incantations you have attuned with another you know, “resolve” amount of times per short rest, without having to rest at a kindled bonfire.

### **Holy Aid**

Prerequisites: None.  
Cost: 2 Trait Points.  
Any type of healing, whether healing allies or yourself, you heal an extra 1d6.

### **Chosen One**

Prerequisites: None.  
Cost: 3 Trait Points.  
Whenever you cast an incantation, the damage it deals or the effect it produces does an extra 1d6 or lasts 1.5x longer, respectively.

### **Divine Aid**

Prerequisites: Holy Aid.  
Cost: 3 Trait Points.  
When casting any form of incantation, it takes 6 less aether to cast.

### **Adept Caster**

Prerequisites: Advanced Memory.  
Cost: 2 Trait Points.  
Gain 2 + “resolve” extra attunement slots.

### **God-Like Blood**

Prerequisites: Adept Caster, Divine Aid.  
Cost: 4 Trait Points.  
Your resolve is now capped at 7 instead of 6. This takes 6 trait points to increase from 6 to 7.

### **The Saviour**

Prerequisites: Chosen One, Divine Aid.  
Cost: 4 Trait Points.  
Similarly to “Chosen One” except the damage increase is 2d6 and effect’s increase is 2x longer. Also, incantations take 10 less aether to cast.
