## **Overview**

**Once, these individuals were deemed divine: their horns were a mark of being chosen. But over time, they were cast out, shunned from society, believed to have tainted blood, and their horns plagued by wraiths.**

## **Traits**

### **Indomitable Figure**

Prerequisites: None.  
Cost: 2 Trait Points.  
Gain +1 to the Endurance and Intimidation skills. These extra points can exceed the regular cap for skills.

### **Horned Charge**

Prerequisites: None.  
Cost: 3 Trait Points.  
Make a charge forward at 10m per tick with 11 priority. This charge does 1d8 damage and leaves the target dazed, giving them -3 to their priority for the active turn. This does 2 vigor to the user as long as they make contact with an obstruction. This can be done twice per short rest.

### **Wraith Barrage**

Prerequisites: None.  
Cost: 3 Trait Points.  
Let out 3 wraiths from your horns that automatically go towards your nearest opponent, dealing 1d4 damage for each wraith that makes contact. This can be done once per short rest.

### **Reinforced Skin**

Prerequisites: Indomitable Figure.  
Cost: 2 Trait Points.  
Your natural skin has become tougher as your horns grow wildly. Your DR increases by 1 permanently.

### **Unrelenting Wraiths**

Prerequisites: Wraith Barrage.  
Cost: 3 Trait Points.  
When using Wraith Barrage, you release 5 wraiths which now do 1d6 damage. You can also now do this two times per short rest.

### **Steel-Like Skin**

Prerequisites: Reinforced Skin.  
Cost: 3 Trait Points.  
Your natural skin has become tougher as your horns grow wildly. Your DR increases by 2 permanently.

### **Wraith Imbuement**

Prerequisites: Unrelenting Wraiths, Horned Charge.  
Cost: 4 Trait Points.  
You may imbue your weapon with the wraiths that secrete from your body, allowing any attacks with your weapon do an extra 2d6 damage.
