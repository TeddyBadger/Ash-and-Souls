## **Overview**

**A race born of rot and decay that once began as ashen, but has now adapted to live in tandem with scarlet rot. You have evolved to harness these deadly elements, and use them to your advantage. Although you have became one with it, the rot always manages to take part of you first: You choose a limb to sacrifice, and in the coming of adulthood, your culture ushers you to craft a prosthetic to replace your lost limb.**

## **Traits**

### **Natural Resistance**

Prerequisites: None.  
Cost: 2 Trait Points.  
You have double the resistance to sleep, poison, scarlet rot, madness and death blight compared to other people, and damage received from any of these effects is halved.
### **Decaying Touch**

Prerequisites: None.  
Cost: 2 Trait Points.  
For two turns, you may imbue your weapon with poison, causing it to do an extra 1d4 damage, and does 20 extra poison build up. If your weapon already applied poison, the 20 would be added to its natural build up, otherwise it simply gets 20 build up. This can be done "vitality" amount of times per short rest and is a half action.
### **Whispers of Aeonia**

Prerequisites: None.  
Cost: 3 Trait Points.  
You can commune with the Aeonian butterflies that inhabit all corners of the world. This can be used to find information, track foes or even scout an area.
### **Putrid Immunity**

Prerequisites: Natural Resistance.  
Cost: 3 Trait Points.  
You are completely immune to sleep, poison, scarlet rot, madness and death blight.
### **Harbinger of Rot**

Prerequisites: Decaying Touch.  
Cost: 3 Trait Points.  
For two turns, you may imbue your weapon with scarlet rot, causing it to do an extra 1d6 damage, and does 20 extra rot build up. If your weapon already applied rot, the 20 would be added to its natural build up, otherwise it simply gets 20 build up. This can be done "vitality" amount of times per short rest and is a half action.
### **Nature's Plague**

Prerequisites: Whispers of Aeonia.  
Cost: 3 Trait Points.  
You may cause a cluster of Aeonian butterflies to release a pulse of rot at the click of your fingers. This pulse does 1d6 damage and causes 30 scarlet rot build up. This can be done "vitality" amount of times per short rest, and is a half action.
### **Potent Affliction**

Prerequisites: Putrid Immunity, Harbinger of Rot, Nature's Plague.  
Cost: 4 Trait Points.  
Whenever you cause build up of sleep, poison, scarlet rot, madness or death blight, increase the build up amount by 10 + vitality.
