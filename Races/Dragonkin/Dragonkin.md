## **Traits**
### **Rough Skin**

Prerequisites: None.  
Cost: 2 Trait Points.  
Your skin is covered in scales, meaning you have a greater natural defence than that of most other people. You have an extra 1 DR.

### **Elemental Lineage**

Prerequisites: None.  
Cost: 2 Trait Points.  
You learn from your heritage, being able to hone your draconic powers into a chosen field. Pick from Fire, Lightning, Magic, Rot, Cold or Occult. Any damage dealt that is the same element does an extra 2d4 damage.

### **Quaking Roar**

Prerequisites: None.  
Cost: 2 Trait Points.  
Let out a ground shaking roar that covers 10m around the user, dealing 1d6 damage to anyone in the area that doesn’t have any noise protection.

### **Imbued Scales**

Prerequisites: Rough Skin.  
Cost: 3 Trait Points.  
As you learn to control your elemental energy, you have unlocked the ability to channel the energy into your scales. For three turns, any physical attack that is made on you causes the attacker to take 2 damage of that element type. This can be activated once per short rest.

### **Razor Claws**

Prerequisites: Elemental Lineage.  
Cost: 2 Trait Points.  
You may attack with your sharp claws at 12 priority, inflicting 1d10 damage of the chosen element.

### **Tail Guard**

Prerequisites: Imbued Scales.  
Cost: 2 Trait Points.  
You may take a guarding stance using your tail with priority 15, any damage received during this stance is reduced by 6, however you cannot exit this stance until at least 8 ticks have passed.

### **Draconic Outrage**

Prerequisites: Elemental Lineage, Quaking Roar.  
Cost: 4 Trait Points.  
By focusing your primal energy into your throat, you can unleash a blast of your chosen element, dealing 2d10 damage in a cone shape in front of you. This has priority 14 and has a maximum range of 8m and 70 degrees.
